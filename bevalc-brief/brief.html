Sazerac commits about $605M across four Kentucky sites, led by $557M at its new Garrard County distillery

T2Courier Journal (KEDFA filings)3 Nov

Sazerac's plans, presented at the 29 Oct Kentucky Economic Development Finance Authority meeting, come with about $5.6M in state tax incentives. The largest piece is $557M at the shuttered Lancaster distillery it bought for $20M in August, expected to create 85 jobs. The rest is $29.4M for a new craft bottling operation at Buffalo Trace (40 jobs), $17.5M at Glenmore in Owensboro (50 jobs) and $2.1M at Cumberland Cooperage (75 jobs). CEO Jake Wenz said in August that Sazerac needs "more supply to keep up with demand."

Why it matters: While much of the bourbon industry is cutting production, the largest private US spirits supplier is adding capacity. Expect more Sazerac allocation volume, and new craft-bottled releases out of Buffalo Trace.
Courier Journal (via Bigdata.com)

Sazerac buys Oregon's Full Sail Brewing for RTD capacity and closes the Hood River brewpub

T2Willamette Week · The Oregonian · BevNET3 Nov

Sazerac, which owns BuzzBallz, told The Oregonian that the deal "enables us to add additional capacity and capabilities to our network." The brewpub closed abruptly and its staff were laid off. A Full Sail brewer posted that the site is now a "100% contract production facility." Full Sail had been owned by Encore Consumer Capital since 2015.

Why it matters: Spirits companies are buying underused craft breweries to make RTDs. Distributors carrying Full Sail Amber and Session should expect brand changes.
Willamette Week (via Bigdata.com) · BevNET (via Bigdata.com)

Hemp-THC: synthetic cannabinoids lose hemp status 12 Nov, most THC drinks 11 Dec; another delay "not out of the question"

T2CNN · New Jersey 101.5 · Forbes3–4 Nov

CNN reports the federal 0.4 mg-per-container limit takes effect 11 December for most hemp products, while delta-8, delta-10 and other synthetic products are banned from 12 November. A bipartisan bill from Sens. Rand Paul and Amy Klobuchar would let states opt out. New Jersey legislators are moving to tie the state's 13 Nov cutoff for alcohol licensees to the federal date. Producer Crescent Canna is moving more than 700 pallets of finished product and selling 50-case freight orders ahead of the deadline.

Why it matters: Retailers and distributors that carry THC seltzers have two dates to plan around: 12 Nov for synthetics (and for Illinois, under its own law) and 11 Dec for everything else.
CNN (via Bigdata.com) · NJ 101.5 (via Bigdata.com) · Forbes (via Bigdata.com)

A-B CEO exit lands as AB InBev reportedly puts $600M into US breweries

T3The Drinks Business3 Nov

Brendan Whitworth leaves at the end of November to run Equinox, and AB InBev CEO Michel Doukeris will lead the North American business in the interim. The Drinks Business reports AB InBev is investing about $600M in its US brewing facilities. Global Q3 revenue was $16.5B, up 4.9%. Beer volume fell 0.1% while non-beer volume rose 4%.

Why it matters: A-B's wholesaler network faces a leadership change while A-B pushes further into non-beer brands (BeatBox, Cutwater, Nütrl), which shifts what its houses sell.
The Drinks Business (via Bigdata.com)

South Africa Wine: losing AGOA on 1 Jan 2027 pushes the US tariff on SA wine toward about 16%

T1South Africa Wine statement · Engineering News3 NovNEW SOURCE

South African wine went from duty-free under AGOA to tariffs of 30%, then 10% and 12.5%. Losing AGOA adds about three points. The US has fallen from SA's third- to seventh-largest packaged-wine market by value, and bulk wine exports to the US fell 79% in 2025 to R13M. The group says exporters and US importers have been sharing the added cost but "cannot continue absorbing additional costs indefinitely."

Why it matters: Importers of South African wine will reprice for 2027. Expect price increases or dropped SKUs, mostly at entry price points.
Engineering News (via Bigdata.com)

Local-option alcohol votes on Tuesday's ballots in North Carolina and Tennessee

T2Public Radio East · WECT · Local 3 News3 NovNEW SOURCE

In eastern North Carolina, Jones County, Pollocksville and Grifton voted on allowing mixed drinks and expanded beer and wine sales. A similar Jones County proposal failed in the early 2000s. Columbus County, NC voted on wine, malt beverage and mixed-beverage sales, and historically dry Meigs County, TN voted on liquor by the drink and package stores. Results weren't published as of this edition.

Why it matters: Approvals open new on-premise and retail license opportunities in these markets. Results go in tomorrow's edition.
Public Radio East · WECT

Woodford Reserve adds Double Oaked Rye to its permanent range, rolling out nationwide

T3The Spirits Business3 Nov

Brown-Forman's new expression is Kentucky straight rye re-barreled for up to 12 months in heavily toasted, lightly charred new oak. It is 45.1% ABV with a $54.99 suggested retail for 750ml, and will be available "soon nationwide."

Why it matters: A new permanent SKU from a top-selling bourbon brand. Distributor catalogs and retailer resets will need to add it.

Wine

Concha y Toro: US business "normalising" after distributor switch; company guides to 2027 sales rebound

T3Drinks International3 Nov

Q3 sales fell 2.2% to CLP 245.6B on a stronger peso and a tough UK market, while net income rose 2.6%. The company says the US "delivered positive results as operations continued to normalise following a distributor transition." It projects 2027 sales growth of 1.9% to 4.7%.

Why it matters: This is a large-supplier example of how long a distributor change takes to settle, a useful reference as RNDC brands move to new houses on 1 January.
Drinks International (via Bigdata.com)

Barrel maker Oeneo: aging division down 15.8% in the half, with the US and Cognac weakest

T1bOeneo H1 release3 NovNEW SOURCE

Revenue for the barrels and casks division was €31.0M, down from €36.8M. Q2 was down 13.9%. Oeneo says spirits producers in particular are postponing investment, and full results are due 3 December.

Why it matters: Producers cutting barrel orders now means less new whiskey and oaked wine coming to market in future years.
Oeneo release (via Bigdata.com)

T3The Spirits Business3 Nov

Three panels judged fruit liqueurs, vermouth, absinthe and aperitivos blind in London. The Spirits Business notes US liqueurs and cordials were flat in volume but up nearly 10% in value in 2025, per DISCUS, helped by lower-ABV and daytime occasions.
