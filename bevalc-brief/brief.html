Reyes takes Bacardi in 11 markets from RNDC, effective 1 Jan 2027

T3The Spirits Business · BevNET29 Oct

Reyes Beverage Group will distribute Bacardi's portfolio (Bacardí, Patrón, Grey Goose, Dewar's, St-Germain) in AZ, CA, CO, DC, FL, HI, LA, MD, OK, SC and TX. Southern Glazer's keeps Bacardi in 36 states and remains its largest US distributor by volume. Reyes has already picked up Pernod in MD and DC and Brown-Forman spirits in California from RNDC. Columbia Distributing closed its purchase of RNDC's Alaska assets the same week.

Why it matters: Reyes, a beer wholesaler, is now a top-tier spirits house in 11 markets. Retailer ordering relationships, distributor catalogs and pricing feeds for these brands change hands on 1 January.
The Spirits Business (via Bigdata.com) · Bacardi release via BevNET

Hemp-THC drinks: 11 Dec federal cutoff holds; Illinois pulls them from shelves 12 Nov

T2CNN · Forbes · CSP · NJ 101.5 · JD Supra28 Oct – 4 Nov

The federal hemp redefinition (0.4 mg total THC per container) takes effect 11 December unless Congress acts again. CNN reports another extension is "not out of the question." Illinois's own law still removes these drinks from mainstream retail on 12 November, and Binny's and other retailers are clearing inventory. New Jersey bills S.4635/A.5604 would move the state's 13 Nov cutoff to match the federal date. Rhode Island has told licensees to remove products before 11 Dec. Producers such as Crescent Canna are moving pallets to states with friendlier laws.

Why it matters: Many alcohol distributors and retailers now carry these SKUs. Three different dates (12 Nov IL, 13 Nov NJ, 11 Dec federal) mean listings have to be removed state by state.
CNN · CSP Daily News · NJ 101.5 · Forbes (all via Bigdata.com)

Judge bars Constellation from dropping Coors Distributing in Denver until arbitration

T2Denver Post · Law360 · Brewbound29–31 Oct

A Denver judge temporarily blocked Constellation Brands from ending its 18-year contract with Coors Distributing Co. for Corona and Modelo. Constellation tried to terminate without cause in September. Coors told the court the loss would cost it more than $100 million, and the judge cited the risk of "reputational harm and termination of other distribution agreements." An arbitrator will decide whether the termination is allowed.

Why it matters: This is a live test of how much freedom suppliers have to move brands between distributors at a time when many distributor networks are being reshuffled.
Denver Post · Law360 (via Bigdata.com)

RNDC wind-down continues: KEG 1 closes Kentucky deal; creditor settlement moves Chapter 11 liquidation forward

T3BevNET · Brewbound2 NovHEADLINE ONLYNEW SOURCE

KEG 1 has completed its purchase of RNDC's Kentucky business, while a joint-venture dispute is still unresolved. A separate report says RNDC has settled with unsecured creditors, which moves its Chapter 11 liquidation forward. RNDC filed for Chapter 11 in July. Breakthru withdrew from its own Kentucky/Indiana deal in August.

Why it matters: Kentucky is the last big open question in RNDC's breakup. Suppliers and retailers there are still waiting to learn who their distributor will be long-term.
BevNET (via Bigdata.com)

Anheuser-Busch North America CEO Brendan Whitworth to leave at end of November

T3BevNET2 NovNEW SOURCE

Whitworth is leaving to lead Equinox. Until a successor is named, North American leadership reports to AB InBev CEO Michel Doukeris. In Circana-tracked off-premise data through 4 Oct, A-B dollar sales are down 0.1% to about $10.7B and volume is down 1.5%. Bud Light lost $183M while Michelob Ultra gained $179.2M.

Why it matters: A leadership change at the largest US brewer while its wholesaler network is expanding into spirits and RTDs.
BevNET (via Bigdata.com)

DISCUS: Canada has fallen from #2 to #6 export market for US spirits; council presses for permanent zero-for-zero tariffs

T1 → T3DISCUS via The Spirits Business30 Oct

DISCUS's Rob Maron says US spirits imports fell from a $12.1B peak in 2022 to $8.4B in 2025 as tariffs took hold. The 51 zero-for-zero partner countries took 81% of US spirits exports in 2025. The US ban on Canadian spirits imports took effect 29 Sept, with exemptions for bulk Canadian whisky and cordials. A Kentucky distiller told CTV that sales lost in Canada have not come back.

Why it matters: Imported Canadian brands are now off US shelves, and bourbon producers are pushing harder in the domestic market to make up lost exports.
The Spirits Business (via Bigdata.com) · BNN Bloomberg

US to end South Africa's AGOA trade preferences 1 Jan 2027; SA wine tariff approaches ~16%

T1South Africa Wine statement3 NovNEW SOURCE

South African wine went from duty-free to 30%, then 10% and 12.5%. Losing AGOA would bring the combined rate close to 16%. The US has fallen from SA's #3 to #7 packaged-wine export market by value, and SA bulk wine exports to the US fell 79% in 2025.

Why it matters: Importers of SA wine will need to reprice for 2027, and some SKUs may be discontinued.
South Africa Wine statement, 3 Nov (original release link to be added; Politicsweb copy blocked)

Glass Packaging Institute warns 50% Section 338 tariff on Canadian glass hits US wine and spirits supply

T1GPI statement28 OctNEW SOURCE

The US imported 348M glass bottles and jars from Canada in 2025, worth more than $86M, all previously duty-free under USMCA. GPI is part of the Toasts Not Tariffs coalition calling for a quick resolution with Canada.

Why it matters: Packaging costs are another source of pressure on supplier prices in 2027.
GPI (via Bigdata.com)

DtC spirits shipping: 68% of adults want it expanded; California's law expires at year-end

T1Sovos ShipCompliant / ACSA · The Spirits Business · Drinks Business28–29 Oct

Only 10 states plus DC allow DtC spirits shipping (AK, AZ, CA, KY, NE, NH, NY, ND, RI, VT), compared with 49 plus DC for wine. That number drops when California's DtC spirits law expires at the end of 2026. Among regular craft spirits drinkers, 81% want the laws changed. The survey was fielded by Harris Poll, 17–19 Aug, n=2,001.

Why it matters: Craft producers selling in California lose this route on 1 January, which matters for Gov Affairs tracking 2027 state sessions.
Sovos release · The Spirits Business (via Bigdata.com)
