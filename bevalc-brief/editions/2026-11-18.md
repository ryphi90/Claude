### **NEW TODAY** Hemp-THC: Illinois and New Jersey bans in force; Total Wine lobbies for a reprieve before the 11 Dec federal cutoff
_T1 · Illinois Dept. of Revenue · NJ Division of Taxation · Drinks Intel · Axios · Chicago Sun-Times · CNN · Forbes · CSP · Marijuana Moment · Oct – 17 Nov_

Illinois began enforcing the Illinois Hemp Act on 12 November "regardless of federal timing." Hemp products over 0.4 mg total THC per container, drinks included, can now only be sold by licensed dispensaries, and the Department of Revenue warns of seizure plus tax, penalty and interest. Binny's and other liquor stores cleared THC drinks ahead of the date, per Axios. Hemp businesses asked a court to block the law, per the Chicago Sun-Times, but no ruling has been reported. New Jersey's Division of Taxation says that from 14 November wholesalers and liquor stores may no longer sell intoxicating hemp beverages, which moves to licensed cannabis retailers. Federally, CNN reports synthetic cannabinoids lost hemp status on 12 November, and everything over 0.4 mg follows on 11 December. Marijuana Moment reports that lobbyists for Anheuser-Busch, Molson Coors and Diageo urged state attorneys general to oppose a federal delay. WSWA favors regulation over a ban. New on 16 Nov: Drinks Intel reports Total Wine & More is lobbying to keep selling THC drinks past next month's federal deadline, with its CEO arguing they belong in the three-tier system. New on 17 Nov: Shanken News Daily reports hemp THC drinks are still gaining share despite the December ban.

> **Why it matters:** Illinois and New Jersey accounts must now be clear of hemp-THC drinks, so distributors should be processing returns. Every other state follows on 11 December.

Sources: [Illinois Dept. of Revenue bulletin FY 2027-04](https://tax.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/tax/research/publications/bulletins/documents/2027/fy-2027-04.pdf) · [NJ Division of Taxation](https://nj.gov/treasury/taxation/hempbev.shtml) · [Drinks Intel: Total Wine](https://drinks-intel.com/cross-category/total-wine-more-lobbies-for-hemp-thc-beverage-reprieve/) · [Shanken News Daily: THC drinks](https://www.shankennewsdaily.com/2026/11/17/41283/thc-drinks-remain-strong-draw-despite-upcoming-ban/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/chicago/2026/11/05/thc-drinks-illinois-liquor-stores-ban) · [Chicago Sun-Times](https://chicago.suntimes.com/small-business/2026/11/07/chicago-businesses-bracing-state-hemp-thc-ban) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/03/health/federal-hemp-thc-product-ban-wellness) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ajherrington/2026/11/03/federal-hemp-thc-ban-forces-beverage-makers-to-prepare/) · [CSP Daily News](https://www.cspdailynews.com/cbdhemp/illinois-retailers-still-face-nov-12-deadline-pull-hemp-derived-thc-beverages) · [Marijuana Moment](https://www.marijuanamoment.net/alcohol-lobbyists-encouraged-law-enforcement-officials-to-push-for-federal-hemp-crackdown-report-says/)

### Anheuser-Busch to buy Straub Distributing, adding a 9-million-case Southern California wholesaler
_T3 · Shanken News Daily · 16 Nov_

A-B plans to acquire Anaheim-based Straub Distributing, which handles nine million cases a year, expanding its company-owned distribution in Southern California. The deal is expected to close early next year. Terms weren't disclosed.

> **Why it matters:** A-B is pulling more of its route to market in-house in one of the largest US beer markets. Suppliers and retailers served by Straub should expect changes to portfolios and programming after closing.

Sources: [Shanken News Daily](https://www.shankennewsdaily.com/2026/11/16/41270/news-briefs-for-november-16-2026/)

### **NEW TODAY** Suntory extends the Jim Beam Clermont distillery pause through most of 2027
_T3 · Drinks Intel · Just Drinks · 17 Nov_

Suntory Global Spirits will keep its Clermont, Kentucky distillery idle into next year. CEO Greg Hughes told the Lexington Herald-Leader the work will take "the better part of 2027." Production has moved to the Booker Noe distillery, and Suntory says there will be no layoffs.

> **Why it matters:** The world's best-selling bourbon is cutting production while US whiskey demand is soft and inventories are high. Expect continued pressure on bourbon pricing and programming.

Sources: [Drinks Intel](https://drinks-intel.com/spirits/suntory-global-spirits-to-extend-jim-beam-production-pause/) · [Just Drinks](https://www.just-drinks.com/news/suntory-extends-jim-beam-pause/)

## Beer & RTD

### AB InBev puts more money into Cutwater production, including $1.5M at its San Diego plant
_T3 · Just Drinks · Shanken News Daily · The Spirits Business · 12–16 Nov_

AB InBev is investing $1.5 million in the San Diego plant that makes Cutwater's cream-based cocktails, part of more than $5 million in Cutwater production spending. Jake Kirsch, president of its Beyond Beer unit, called Cutwater "the number-one spirits-based cocktail brand in the US." New on 13 Nov: Shanken reports Cutwater's dollar sales grew 13% in the four weeks to 31 October, and frames the investment as building Cutwater's innovation hub as RTD competition intensifies. The Spirits Business adds that the money expands cream-based cocktail production ahead of the holidays and will create new manufacturing jobs at the plant, which already employs more than 200 people.

> **Why it matters:** A-B keeps adding capacity for spirits-based RTDs, which its beer wholesalers carry.

Sources: [Just Drinks](https://just-drinks.com/news/ab-inbev-invests-again-in-cutwater-production) · [Shanken News Daily](https://www.shankennewsdaily.com/2026/11/13/41266/a-b-investing-in-cutwaters-innovation-hub-as-rtd-competition-continues-to-intensify/) · [The Spirits Business](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2026/11/ab-inbev-pours-1-5m-into-cutwater-production/)
