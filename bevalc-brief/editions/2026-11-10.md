### **NEW TODAY** Hemp-THC: Illinois enforces its own ban 12 Nov; federal cutoff 11 Dec (synthetics 12 Nov); NJ bill awaits governor
_T1 · Illinois Dept. of Revenue · Axios · Chicago Sun-Times · CNN · NJ 101.5 · Forbes · CSP · Marijuana Moment · Oct – 7 Nov · NEW SOURCE_

Illinois's Department of Revenue says it will start enforcing the Illinois Hemp Act on 12 November "regardless of federal timing." Hemp-THC drinks over 0.4 mg per container must leave mainstream retail and can only be sold through licensed dispensaries. The bulletin warns of product seizure plus tax, penalty and interest, and tells retailers to stop ordering non-compliant products and return existing stock to distributors. CSP reports Illinois c-store chains have stopped reordering. New this week: Axios reports Binny's Beverage Depot and other liquor stores are already clearing out THC drinks, and the Chicago Sun-Times reports the Illinois Healthy Alternatives Association and a group of hemp businesses have filed an emergency motion for a temporary restraining order, arguing the law is unconstitutional. No ruling has been reported, so 12 Nov stands for now. Federally, CNN reports synthetic cannabinoids lose hemp status 12 Nov and everything over 0.4 mg 11 Dec, and another extension is "not out of the question." New Jersey's S4635, which would move its 13 Nov cutoff to the federal date, passed both houses and awaits the governor. Forbes reports producer Crescent Canna is moving more than 700 pallets out of North Carolina. New on 4 Nov: Marijuana Moment, citing emails obtained by Politico, reports that lobbyists for Anheuser-Busch, Molson Coors and Diageo urged state attorneys general to sign the letter asking Congress not to delay the ban. Attorneys general from 34 states and one territory signed it. WSWA, by contrast, favors regulation over a ban.

> **Why it matters:** Illinois accounts have to be cleared by 12 Nov, a month before the federal date, so distributors should plan for returns now. Elsewhere, 12 Nov applies to synthetics and 11 Dec to everything else.

Sources: [Illinois Dept. of Revenue bulletin FY 2027-04](https://tax.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/tax/research/publications/bulletins/documents/2027/fy-2027-04.pdf) · [CSP Daily News](https://www.cspdailynews.com/cbdhemp/illinois-retailers-still-face-nov-12-deadline-pull-hemp-derived-thc-beverages) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/chicago/2026/11/05/thc-drinks-illinois-liquor-stores-ban) · [Chicago Sun-Times](https://chicago.suntimes.com/small-business/2026/11/07/chicago-businesses-bracing-state-hemp-thc-ban) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/03/health/federal-hemp-thc-product-ban-wellness) · [NJ 101.5](https://nj1015.com/new-jersey-hemp-thc-drinks-deadline/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ajherrington/2026/11/03/federal-hemp-thc-ban-forces-beverage-makers-to-prepare/) · [Marijuana Moment](https://www.marijuanamoment.net/alcohol-lobbyists-encouraged-law-enforcement-officials-to-push-for-federal-hemp-crackdown-report-says/)

### RNDC wind-down: JV partner sues over Kentucky sale proceeds after liquidation plan approval
_T3 · The Spirits Business · Distribution Strategy Group · Fingers · Inc. · Brewbound · BevNET · 2–5 Nov_

The Southern District of Texas bankruptcy court approved RNDC's second amended Chapter 11 liquidation plan on 27 October, which sets up the wind-down without closing the case. KEG 1 River City completed its purchase of RNDC's Kentucky assets effective 30 October, about 2 million cases a year, after Breakthru pulled out. New on 5 Nov: National Wine & Spirits, RNDC's partner in the Kentucky joint venture, is challenging a proposed $6.57 million payment to RNDC's parent from the Kentucky sale proceeds and wants the money held until its own claim is decided. That claim, filed 29 October, says RNDC breached its duties to the joint venture during its exit, including ignoring supplier complaints and paying suppliers late, and destroyed about $185 million of the venture's value. KEG 1 says the dispute won't affect its Kentucky operations. Columbia Distributing closed its purchase of RNDC's Alaska assets on 26 October, adding 1,500 accounts. As part of the plan, the unsecured creditors committee, which includes major suppliers such as Proximo and Delicato, won $47 million of a $64.5 million reserve that had been set aside for employees' deferred compensation. Inc. reports the committee argued the claims released under the plan could be worth more than $1 billion.

> **Why it matters:** RNDC's breakup is nearly complete. Kentucky and Alaska suppliers and retailers now have new long-term distributors, and the Kentucky JV dispute is the main open risk.

Sources: [The Spirits Business](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2026/11/rndc-faces-lawsuit-over-proposed-us6-6m-kentucky-payment/) · [Distribution Strategy Group](https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/11/rndc-wins-court-approval-of-liquidation-plan-as-columbia-closes-alaska-acquisition) · [Fingers](https://www.fingers.email/p/a-particularly-nasty-detail-in-rndc-s-liquidation-plan) · [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/lucia-auerbach/biggest-liquor-distributors-faces-300-million-dollars-claims-not-biggest-bankruptcy-fight/91426038) · [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/keg-1-completes-rndc-acquisition-as-joint-venture-dispute-lingers) · [BevNET](https://www.bevnet.com/spirits/2026/after-breakthru-walks-keg-1-moves-on-rndcs-kentucky-business)

### Molson Coors Q3: volume down 3%, full-year outlook cut
_T1b · Molson Coors Q3 release · 5 Nov_

Net sales fell 1.6% (−1.2% in constant currency) to $2,926.5M as financial volume dropped 3.0%, with lower shipments in both the Americas and EMEA & APAC. Net income was $271.5M, or $1.45 a share, and underlying EPS was $1.46. Molson Coors now expects 2026 net sales down 1% to 2% in constant currency, underlying pre-tax income down 21% to 24%, and underlying EPS down 16% to 19%.

> **Why it matters:** The second-largest US brewer is cutting its outlook as beer demand stays soft. Expect tighter programming and pricing pressure through its wholesaler network into 2027.

Sources: [Molson Coors release (Business Wire)](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-11-5-molson-coors-beverage-company-reports-2026-third-quarter-results)

### Coors Distributing fights two supplier exits: Constellation held until arbitration, Mark Anthony case sent to arbitration
_T3 · Brewbound · Law360 · 29 Oct – 5 Nov_

A Colorado judge granted Coors Distributing Co. a preliminary injunction that keeps Constellation Brands' Corona and Modelo in its portfolio until the two arbitrate. Constellation had tried to end the 18-year agreement without cause, and Coors argued the exit risked other suppliers leaving too. New on 5 Nov: a second supplier, Mark Anthony Brands (White Claw), and Coors Distributing jointly asked to move their own contract-exit case to arbitration, and the judge granted it. That is the 2 Nov agreement Law360 reported.

> **Why it matters:** Two major suppliers are trying to leave the same large wholesaler. These cases test how freely suppliers can move brands under Colorado law as distributor networks reshuffle.

Sources: [Brewbound: Mark Anthony](https://www.brewbound.com/news/coors-distributing-mark-anthony-file-jointly-to-move-case-to-arbitration) · [Brewbound: Constellation](https://www.brewbound.com/news/judge-orders-constellation-brands-and-coors-distributing-company-to-arbitration/) · [Law360: injunction](https://www.law360.com/articles/2544167) · [Law360: arbitration](https://www.law360.com/articles/2545252/coors-supplier-send-colo-contract-exit-suit-to-arbitration)

### Diageo's first quarter beats expectations, but North America is still declining
_T1b · Diageo Q1 FY27 trading statement · The Spirits Business · 5 Nov_

For the quarter to 30 September, Diageo's reported net sales rose 1.2% to $4,931M, and organic net sales grew 1.4% (0.6% excluding EABL). Diageo says North America organic net sales fell mid-single digits on continued market softness; The Spirits Business puts the decline at 3.6%. Full-year guidance is unchanged: Diageo still expects North America organic net sales to fall mid-single digits, assuming the US spirits market declines about 3%, with better share performance than last year.

> **Why it matters:** The largest spirits supplier expects another down year in the US. Distributors and retailers should plan Diageo volume and programming accordingly.

Sources: [Diageo release](https://www.diageo.com/en/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/diageo-issues-fiscal-27-q1-trading-statement) · [The Spirits Business](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2026/11/diageo-q1-2027-sales-rise-beats-expectations/)

### **NEW TODAY** Election Day: voters approve 19 local measures expanding spirits access in seven states, including NC, NJ and Mississippi
_T2 · DISCUS · Public Radio East · WWAY · FOX 29 · WLOX · 4–6 Nov_

DISCUS counts 19 local ballot measures expanding spirits sales approved across seven states, including Sunday sales and on- and off-premise sales. In North Carolina, Jones County approved countywide mixed-beverage sales 2,512 to 1,251, covering unincorporated areas and towns that hadn't already authorized them. Grifton and Pollocksville passed their own town measures. Columbus County passed its measures narrowly: mixed beverages 54.56%, malt beverages 50.78% and unfortified wine 50.63%. Businesses still need state permits before they can serve. In New Jersey, Collingswood voters ended more than a century as a dry town; state population limits cap it at one liquor-store license or four on-premise licenses, and the borough still has to set zoning, hours and fees. In Mississippi, Pearl River County voters passed two referendums expanding alcohol sales.

> **Why it matters:** These are new on-premise and retail license markets. Expect permit applications from restaurants, bars and stores in the coming months.

Sources: [DISCUS](https://distilledspirits.org/news/voters-across-seven-states-approve-19-local-measures-expanding-access-to-distilled-spirits) · [Public Radio East](https://www.publicradioeast.org/2026-11-04/voters-approve-expanded-alcohol-sales-in-grifton-jones-county-and-pollocksville) · [WWAY](https://www.wwaytv3.com/columbus-county-voters-narrowly-approve-expanded-alcohol-sales-in-referendum/) · [FOX 29](https://www.fox29.com/news/collingswood-ends-more-than-100-years-dry-town-2026-election-vote) · [WLOX](https://www.wlox.com/2026/11/04/pearl-river-county-alcohol-referendums-pass-expanding-sales-county/)
