# Bev-Alc Morning Brief

_US Three-Tier · Daily Brief · Tue 3 Nov 2026_

## Top 5

### Sazerac buys Oregon's Full Sail Brewing for RTD capacity and closes the Hood River brewpub
_T2 · Willamette Week · BevNET · Brewbound · 3 Nov_

Sazerac, which owns BuzzBallz, confirmed the purchase on Tuesday and told The Oregonian the deal "enables us to add additional capacity and capabilities to our network." The brewpub closed abruptly Monday and its restaurant staff were laid off. A Full Sail brewer posted that the site is now a "100% contract production facility." Sazerac hasn't said what happens to the Full Sail brands.

> **Why it matters:** Spirits companies are buying underused craft breweries to make RTDs. Distributors carrying Full Sail Amber and Session should expect brand changes.

Sources: [Willamette Week](https://www.wweek.com/culture/2026/11/03/craft-brewery-full-sail-sells-to-beverage-giant-behind-buzzballz/) · [BevNET](https://www.bevnet.com/spirits/2026/sazerac-acquires-oregons-full-sail-brewing-for-rtd-capacity) · [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/sazerac-acquires-oregons-full-sail-brewing)

### Hemp-THC: synthetics banned 12 Nov, most THC drinks 11 Dec; New Jersey bill to match the federal date awaits the governor
_T2 · CNN · New Jersey 101.5 · Forbes · 3 Nov_

CNN reports that hemp products with synthetic cannabinoids (delta-8, delta-10) are banned from 12 November, and everything over 0.4 mg THC per container from 11 December. A bill from Sens. Rand Paul and Amy Klobuchar would let states opt out, and another extension is "not out of the question." In New Jersey, S4635 passed the Senate 37-0 and the Assembly 75-2. If the governor signs it, the state's 13 Nov cutoff for alcohol licensees moves to the federal date. Forbes reports producer Crescent Canna is moving more than 700 pallets from North Carolina to Tennessee and selling 50-case freight orders ahead of the deadline.

> **Why it matters:** Retailers and distributors carrying THC seltzers have two dates to plan around: 12 Nov for synthetics (and for Illinois, under its own law) and 11 Dec for everything else.

Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/03/health/federal-hemp-thc-product-ban-wellness) · [NJ 101.5](https://nj1015.com/new-jersey-hemp-thc-drinks-deadline/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ajherrington/2026/11/03/federal-hemp-thc-ban-forces-beverage-makers-to-prepare/)

### A-B's Whitworth exits as AB InBev leans on non-beer growth
_T3 · The Drinks Business · AB InBev Q3 6-K · 3 Nov_

Brendan Whitworth leaves at the end of November to become CEO of Equinox, and AB InBev CEO Michel Doukeris will run North America in the interim. AB InBev's Q3 filing shows beer volume down 0.1% and non-beer volume up 4.0%. No-alcohol beer revenue grew 35% and Beyond Beer revenue grew 19%. Reported revenue was about $16.51B (+9.1%), or +4.9% organic.

> **Why it matters:** A-B's wholesaler network faces a leadership change while A-B pushes further into non-beer brands, which shifts what its houses sell.

Sources: [The Drinks Business](https://www.thedrinksbusiness.com/2026/11/anheuser-busch-ceo-departs-as-beer-volumes-slide/) · [AB InBev Q3 release (SEC 6-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001668717/000119312526437051/d159047dex991.htm)

### US to end South Africa's AGOA duty-free access on 1 Jan 2027; wine among exports hit
_T2 · Engineering News · 2 Nov · NEW SOURCE_

US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer said South Africa and Rwanda will be suspended from AGOA from 1 January 2027, citing human rights, corruption and threats to US interests. South Africa's trade ministry says AGOA goods are now about 10% of its exports to the US. South African wine already faces a 30% US tariff from August 2025. Packaged wine exports to the US fell 21% in volume and 23% in value in 2025, per Wines of South Africa.

> **Why it matters:** Importers of South African wine face another cost increase for 2027. Expect repricing or dropped SKUs, mostly at entry price points.

Sources: [Engineering News: AGOA suspension](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/us-to-suspend-south-africa-and-rwanda-from-duty-free-trade-access-2026-11-02) · [Engineering News: 2025 wine exports](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/south-africas-wine-exports-decrease-2026-01-26)

### Woodford Reserve adds Double Oaked Rye to its permanent range, rolling out nationwide
_T3 · The Spirits Business · 3 Nov_

Brown-Forman's new expression is Kentucky straight rye re-barreled for up to 12 months in heavily toasted, lightly charred new oak. It is 45.1% ABV with a $54.99 suggested retail for 750ml, and will be available "soon nationwide." It joins Double Oaked bourbon as a permanent release.

> **Why it matters:** A new permanent SKU from a top-selling bourbon brand. Distributor catalogs and retailer resets will need to add it.

Sources: [The Spirits Business](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2026/11/woodford-reserve-debuts-double-oaked-rye/)

## Wine

### Barrel and closure maker Oeneo: H1 turnover down 5.1%, barrels down 15.8%
_T1b · Oeneo H1 release · 3 Nov · NEW SOURCE_

First-half turnover was €133.1M, down 5.1% (−7.2% at constant scope and exchange rates). Closures were €102.1M (−1.3%). Winemaking, which includes barrels, was €31.0M (−15.8%), with barrels and large vats hit hardest as wine and spirits producers delay investment. Oeneo expects its recurring operating margin to be slightly above 11.0%, versus 14.0% a year ago, and sees no clear sign of market recovery.

> **Why it matters:** Producers cutting barrel orders now means less new whiskey and oaked wine coming to market in future years.

Sources: [Oeneo release (Actusnews)](https://www.actusnews.com/fr/oeneo/cp/2026/11/03/first-half-turnover-eur-133_1-million-sector-challenges-continue-to-weigh-on-business)
