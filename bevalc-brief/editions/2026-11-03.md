### **NEW TODAY** Hemp-THC: Illinois enforces its own ban 12 Nov; federal cutoff 11 Dec (synthetics 12 Nov); NJ bill awaits governor
_T1 · Illinois Dept. of Revenue · CNN · NJ 101.5 · Forbes · CSP · Oct – 3 Nov_

Illinois's Department of Revenue says it will start enforcing the Illinois Hemp Act on 12 November "regardless of federal timing." Hemp-THC drinks over 0.4 mg per container must leave mainstream retail and can only be sold through licensed dispensaries. The bulletin warns of product seizure plus tax, penalty and interest, and tells retailers to stop ordering non-compliant products and return existing stock to distributors. CSP reports Illinois c-store chains have stopped reordering. Federally, CNN reports synthetic cannabinoids lose hemp status 12 Nov and everything over 0.4 mg 11 Dec, and another extension is "not out of the question." New Jersey's S4635, which would move its 13 Nov cutoff to the federal date, passed both houses and awaits the governor. Forbes reports producer Crescent Canna is moving more than 700 pallets out of North Carolina.

> **Why it matters:** Illinois accounts have to be cleared by 12 Nov, a month before the federal date, so distributors should plan for returns now. Elsewhere, 12 Nov applies to synthetics and 11 Dec to everything else.

Sources: [Illinois Dept. of Revenue bulletin FY 2027-04](https://tax.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/tax/research/publications/bulletins/documents/2027/fy-2027-04.pdf) · [CSP Daily News](https://www.cspdailynews.com/cbdhemp/illinois-retailers-still-face-nov-12-deadline-pull-hemp-derived-thc-beverages) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/03/health/federal-hemp-thc-product-ban-wellness) · [NJ 101.5](https://nj1015.com/new-jersey-hemp-thc-drinks-deadline/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ajherrington/2026/11/03/federal-hemp-thc-ban-forces-beverage-makers-to-prepare/)

### **NEW TODAY** Sazerac buys Oregon's Full Sail Brewing for RTD capacity and closes the Hood River brewpub

### Court keeps Constellation's Corona and Modelo with Coors Distributing until arbitration
_T3 · Brewbound · Law360 · 29–30 Oct, 2 Nov_

Coors Distributing Co. won a preliminary injunction that holds Constellation Brands' planned exit from its portfolio until the two companies arbitrate. Constellation tried to end the 18-year agreement without cause. Coors argued the exit posed a "real risk" that other suppliers would leave too. On 2 Nov, Law360 reported Coors and a supplier agreed to send the dispute to an arbitrator.

> **Why it matters:** This is a live test of how freely suppliers can move brands between wholesalers while distributor networks are being reshuffled.

Sources: [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/judge-orders-constellation-brands-and-coors-distributing-company-to-arbitration/) · [Law360: injunction](https://www.law360.com/articles/2544167) · [Law360: arbitration](https://www.law360.com/retail/articles/2545252)

### US to end South Africa's AGOA duty-free access on 1 Jan 2027; wine among exports hit
_T2 · Engineering News · 2 Nov · NEW SOURCE_

US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer said South Africa and Rwanda will be suspended from AGOA from 1 January 2027. South African wine already faces a 30% US tariff. Packaged wine exports to the US fell 21% in volume and 23% in value in 2025, per Wines of South Africa.

> **Why it matters:** Importers of South African wine face another cost increase for 2027. Expect repricing or dropped SKUs.

Sources: [Engineering News: AGOA suspension](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/us-to-suspend-south-africa-and-rwanda-from-duty-free-trade-access-2026-11-02) · [Engineering News: 2025 wine exports](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/south-africas-wine-exports-decrease-2026-01-26)

### DtC spirits shipping: 68% of adults want it expanded; California's law expires at year-end
_T1b · Sovos ShipCompliant / ACSA release · 28 Oct_

Support among adults 21+ is at 68%, the highest in the report's five years, and 81% among regular craft spirits drinkers. Only about 10 states plus DC allow DtC spirits shipping, compared with 49 plus DC for wine. California's authorization expires at the end of 2026 after an extension failed. The survey was fielded by Harris Poll, 17–19 Aug, n=2,001.

> **Why it matters:** Craft producers selling into California lose that channel on 1 January, a 2027 session issue for Gov Affairs.

Sources: [Release](https://www.prweb.com/releases/consumer-demand-for-direct-to-consumer-craft-spirits-shows-staying-power-302921857.html) · [Sovos report summary](https://sovos.com/blog/ship/five-years-of-proof-the-case-for-dtc-spirits-shipping/)

### Glass Packaging Institute: 50% Section 338 tariff on Canadian glass adds strain to US wine and spirits supply
_T1 · Glass Packaging Institute (via American Recycler) · 28 Oct · NEW SOURCE_

GPI, a member of the Toasts Not Tariffs coalition, says it is particularly concerned about the 50% Section 338 tariff on Canadian glass bottles, jars and raw materials. It is urging both governments to resume talks and restore market access for US and Canadian wine and spirits.

> **Why it matters:** Packaging costs are another pressure on supplier pricing for 2027.

Sources: [American Recycler](https://americanrecycler.com/glass-recycling/gpi-us-canada-tariff-dispute/)

## Wine

### NielsenIQ: wine still down 3.8% over four weeks, but sparkling turns positive
_T3 · NielsenIQ via Wine Industry Advisor · 2 Nov_

For the four weeks to 24 October, off-premise wine dollars fell 3.8% and volume fell 5.3%. Weaker consumer engagement caused 73% of the losses. Still wine fell 4.6% in value, sparkling rose 1.5% in dollars, and non-alc wine rose 18.4%. Among suppliers, Gallo was down 1.2%, The Wine Group down 9.4% and Deutsch Family up 3.4%.

> **Why it matters:** Sparkling's growth heading into the holidays makes it the category to push in Q4 retailer conversations.

Sources: [Wine Industry Advisor](https://wineindustryadvisor.com/2026/11/02/seasonal-demand-contributes-to-some-relief-as-four-week-declines-persist-nielseniq/)

### **NEW TODAY** Barrel and closure maker Oeneo: H1 turnover down 5.1%, barrels down 15.8%
_T1b · Oeneo H1 release · 3 Nov_

First-half turnover was €133.1M, down 5.1% (−7.2% at constant scope and exchange rates). Closures were €102.1M (−1.3%). Winemaking, which includes barrels, was €31.0M (−15.8%), with barrels and large vats hit hardest as wine and spirits producers delay investment. Oeneo sees no clear sign of market recovery.

> **Why it matters:** Producers cutting barrel orders now means less new whiskey and oaked wine coming to market in future years.

Sources: [Oeneo release (Actusnews)](https://www.actusnews.com/fr/oeneo/cp/2026/11/03/first-half-turnover-eur-133_1-million-sector-challenges-continue-to-weigh-on-business)
