### Hemp-THC: bipartisan Senate bill would replace the 11 Dec federal ban with FDA rules; Illinois and New Jersey bans in force
_T1 · Illinois Dept. of Revenue · NJ Division of Taxation · Drinks Intel · Axios · Chicago Sun-Times · CNN · Forbes · CSP · Marijuana Moment · Oct – 19 Nov_

Illinois began enforcing the Illinois Hemp Act on 12 November "regardless of federal timing." Hemp products over 0.4 mg total THC per container, drinks included, can now only be sold by licensed dispensaries, and the Department of Revenue warns of seizure plus tax, penalty and interest. Binny's and other liquor stores cleared THC drinks ahead of the date, per Axios. Hemp businesses asked a court to block the law, per the Chicago Sun-Times, but no ruling has been reported. New Jersey's Division of Taxation says that from 14 November wholesalers and liquor stores may no longer sell intoxicating hemp beverages, which moves to licensed cannabis retailers. Federally, CNN reports synthetic cannabinoids lost hemp status on 12 November, and everything over 0.4 mg follows on 11 December. Marijuana Moment reports that lobbyists for Anheuser-Busch, Molson Coors and Diageo urged state attorneys general to oppose a federal delay. WSWA favors regulation over a ban. New on 16 Nov: Drinks Intel reports Total Wine & More is lobbying to keep selling THC drinks past next month's federal deadline, with its CEO arguing they belong in the three-tier system. New on 17 Nov: Shanken News Daily reports hemp THC drinks are still gaining share despite the December ban. New on 19 Nov: Sens. Tim Sheehy and Amy Klobuchar introduced the Hemp Guardrails Act, which would replace the 0.4 mg cap with FDA oversight of hemp-derived THC products, a national 21+ age limit, and bans on synthetic cannabinoids, smokable flower and imports from China, per Marijuana Moment. It has not advanced beyond introduction.

> **Why it matters:** Illinois and New Jersey accounts must now be clear of hemp-THC drinks, so distributors should be processing returns. Every other state follows on 11 December.

Sources: [Illinois Dept. of Revenue bulletin FY 2027-04](https://tax.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/tax/research/publications/bulletins/documents/2027/fy-2027-04.pdf) · [NJ Division of Taxation](https://nj.gov/treasury/taxation/hempbev.shtml) · [Drinks Intel: Total Wine](https://drinks-intel.com/cross-category/total-wine-more-lobbies-for-hemp-thc-beverage-reprieve/) · [Shanken News Daily: THC drinks](https://www.shankennewsdaily.com/2026/11/17/41283/thc-drinks-remain-strong-draw-despite-upcoming-ban/) · [Marijuana Moment: Senate bill](https://www.marijuanamoment.net/bipartisan-senators-file-bill-to-keep-hemp-thc-products-federally-legal-as-next-months-scheduled-ban-looms/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/chicago/2026/11/05/thc-drinks-illinois-liquor-stores-ban) · [Chicago Sun-Times](https://chicago.suntimes.com/small-business/2026/11/07/chicago-businesses-bracing-state-hemp-thc-ban) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/03/health/federal-hemp-thc-product-ban-wellness) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ajherrington/2026/11/03/federal-hemp-thc-ban-forces-beverage-makers-to-prepare/) · [CSP Daily News](https://www.cspdailynews.com/cbdhemp/illinois-retailers-still-face-nov-12-deadline-pull-hemp-derived-thc-beverages) · [Marijuana Moment](https://www.marijuanamoment.net/alcohol-lobbyists-encouraged-law-enforcement-officials-to-push-for-federal-hemp-crackdown-report-says/)

### **NEW TODAY** Kentucky bourbon production fell 29.7% in 2025 to its lowest level since 2019, and the KDA expects a further drop in 2026
_T1 · Kentucky Distillers' Association via Drinks Intel · 23 Nov_

Kentucky distillers filled 2.1 million barrels in 2025, down 29.7% from 3 million in 2024, according to the KDA. The figures come from inventories reported to the state Department of Revenue on 1 January. The KDA expects production to fall again in 2026.

> **Why it matters:** Distillers are cutting output to work through high inventories as US whiskey demand softens. Expect continued pressure on bourbon pricing, and fewer new barrels in future aged releases.

Sources: [Drinks Intel](https://drinks-intel.com/spirits/kentucky-distillers-cut-bourbon-production-by-third-in-2025-data)
