### Hemp-THC: Illinois enforces its own ban 12 Nov; federal cutoff 11 Dec (synthetics 12 Nov); NJ bill awaits governor
_T1 · Illinois Dept. of Revenue · CNN · NJ 101.5 · Forbes · CSP · Marijuana Moment · Oct – 4 Nov_

Illinois's Department of Revenue says it will start enforcing the Illinois Hemp Act on 12 November "regardless of federal timing." Hemp-THC drinks over 0.4 mg per container must leave mainstream retail and can only be sold through licensed dispensaries. The bulletin warns of product seizure plus tax, penalty and interest, and tells retailers to stop ordering non-compliant products and return existing stock to distributors. CSP reports Illinois c-store chains have stopped reordering. Federally, CNN reports synthetic cannabinoids lose hemp status 12 Nov and everything over 0.4 mg 11 Dec, and another extension is "not out of the question." New Jersey's S4635, which would move its 13 Nov cutoff to the federal date, passed both houses and awaits the governor. Forbes reports producer Crescent Canna is moving more than 700 pallets out of North Carolina. New on 4 Nov: Marijuana Moment, citing emails obtained by Politico, reports that lobbyists for Anheuser-Busch, Molson Coors and Diageo urged state attorneys general to sign the letter asking Congress not to delay the ban. Attorneys general from 34 states and one territory signed it. WSWA, by contrast, favors regulation over a ban.

> **Why it matters:** Illinois accounts have to be cleared by 12 Nov, a month before the federal date, so distributors should plan for returns now. Elsewhere, 12 Nov applies to synthetics and 11 Dec to everything else.

Sources: [Illinois Dept. of Revenue bulletin FY 2027-04](https://tax.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/tax/research/publications/bulletins/documents/2027/fy-2027-04.pdf) · [CSP Daily News](https://www.cspdailynews.com/cbdhemp/illinois-retailers-still-face-nov-12-deadline-pull-hemp-derived-thc-beverages) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/03/health/federal-hemp-thc-product-ban-wellness) · [NJ 101.5](https://nj1015.com/new-jersey-hemp-thc-drinks-deadline/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ajherrington/2026/11/03/federal-hemp-thc-ban-forces-beverage-makers-to-prepare/) · [Marijuana Moment](https://www.marijuanamoment.net/alcohol-lobbyists-encouraged-law-enforcement-officials-to-push-for-federal-hemp-crackdown-report-says/)

### RNDC wind-down: JV partner sues over Kentucky sale proceeds after liquidation plan approval
_T3 · The Spirits Business · Distribution Strategy Group · Fingers · Inc. · Brewbound · BevNET · 2–5 Nov_

The Southern District of Texas bankruptcy court approved RNDC's second amended Chapter 11 liquidation plan on 27 October, which sets up the wind-down without closing the case. KEG 1 River City completed its purchase of RNDC's Kentucky assets effective 30 October, about 2 million cases a year, after Breakthru pulled out. New on 5 Nov: National Wine & Spirits, RNDC's partner in the Kentucky joint venture, is challenging a proposed $6.57 million payment to RNDC's parent from the Kentucky sale proceeds and wants the money held until its own claim is decided. That claim, filed 29 October, says RNDC breached its duties to the joint venture during its exit, including ignoring supplier complaints and paying suppliers late, and destroyed about $185 million of the venture's value. KEG 1 says the dispute won't affect its Kentucky operations. Columbia Distributing closed its purchase of RNDC's Alaska assets on 26 October, adding 1,500 accounts. As part of the plan, the unsecured creditors committee, which includes major suppliers such as Proximo and Delicato, won $47 million of a $64.5 million reserve that had been set aside for employees' deferred compensation. Inc. reports the committee argued the claims released under the plan could be worth more than $1 billion.

> **Why it matters:** RNDC's breakup is nearly complete. Kentucky and Alaska suppliers and retailers now have new long-term distributors, and the Kentucky JV dispute is the main open risk.

Sources: [The Spirits Business](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2026/11/rndc-faces-lawsuit-over-proposed-us6-6m-kentucky-payment/) · [Distribution Strategy Group](https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/11/rndc-wins-court-approval-of-liquidation-plan-as-columbia-closes-alaska-acquisition) · [Fingers](https://www.fingers.email/p/a-particularly-nasty-detail-in-rndc-s-liquidation-plan) · [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/lucia-auerbach/biggest-liquor-distributors-faces-300-million-dollars-claims-not-biggest-bankruptcy-fight/91426038) · [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/keg-1-completes-rndc-acquisition-as-joint-venture-dispute-lingers) · [BevNET](https://www.bevnet.com/spirits/2026/after-breakthru-walks-keg-1-moves-on-rndcs-kentucky-business)

### Molson Coors Q3: volume down 3%, full-year outlook cut
_T1b · Molson Coors Q3 release · 5 Nov_

Net sales fell 1.6% (−1.2% in constant currency) to $2,926.5M as financial volume dropped 3.0%, with lower shipments in both the Americas and EMEA & APAC. Net income was $271.5M, or $1.45 a share, and underlying EPS was $1.46. Molson Coors now expects 2026 net sales down 1% to 2% in constant currency, underlying pre-tax income down 21% to 24%, and underlying EPS down 16% to 19%.

> **Why it matters:** The second-largest US brewer is cutting its outlook as beer demand stays soft. Expect tighter programming and pricing pressure through its wholesaler network into 2027.

Sources: [Molson Coors release (Business Wire)](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-11-5-molson-coors-beverage-company-reports-2026-third-quarter-results)

### Coors Distributing fights two supplier exits: Constellation held until arbitration, Mark Anthony case sent to arbitration
_T3 · Brewbound · Law360 · 29 Oct – 5 Nov_

A Colorado judge granted Coors Distributing Co. a preliminary injunction that keeps Constellation Brands' Corona and Modelo in its portfolio until the two arbitrate. Constellation had tried to end the 18-year agreement without cause, and Coors argued the exit risked other suppliers leaving too. New on 5 Nov: a second supplier, Mark Anthony Brands (White Claw), and Coors Distributing jointly asked to move their own contract-exit case to arbitration, and the judge granted it. That is the 2 Nov agreement Law360 reported.

> **Why it matters:** Two major suppliers are trying to leave the same large wholesaler. These cases test how freely suppliers can move brands under Colorado law as distributor networks reshuffle.

Sources: [Brewbound: Mark Anthony](https://www.brewbound.com/news/coors-distributing-mark-anthony-file-jointly-to-move-case-to-arbitration) · [Brewbound: Constellation](https://www.brewbound.com/news/judge-orders-constellation-brands-and-coors-distributing-company-to-arbitration/) · [Law360: injunction](https://www.law360.com/articles/2544167) · [Law360: arbitration](https://www.law360.com/articles/2545252/coors-supplier-send-colo-contract-exit-suit-to-arbitration)

### US to end South Africa's AGOA duty-free access on 1 Jan 2027; wine among exports hit
_T2 · Engineering News · 2 Nov_

US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer said South Africa and Rwanda will be suspended from AGOA from 1 January 2027. South African wine already faces a 30% US tariff. Packaged wine exports to the US fell 21% in volume and 23% in value in 2025, per Wines of South Africa.

> **Why it matters:** Importers of South African wine face another cost increase for 2027. Expect repricing or dropped SKUs.

Sources: [Engineering News: AGOA suspension](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/us-to-suspend-south-africa-and-rwanda-from-duty-free-trade-access-2026-11-02) · [Engineering News: 2025 wine exports](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/south-africas-wine-exports-decrease-2026-01-26)

## Wine

### NielsenIQ: wine still down 3.8% over four weeks, but sparkling turns positive
_T3 · NielsenIQ via Wine Industry Advisor · 2 Nov_

For the four weeks to 24 October, off-premise wine dollars fell 3.8% and volume fell 5.3%. Weaker consumer engagement caused 73% of the losses. Still wine fell 4.6% in value, sparkling rose 1.5% in dollars, and non-alc wine rose 18.4%. Among suppliers, Gallo was down 1.2%, The Wine Group down 9.4% and Deutsch Family up 3.4%.

> **Why it matters:** Sparkling's growth heading into the holidays makes it the category to push in Q4 retailer conversations.

Sources: [Wine Industry Advisor](https://wineindustryadvisor.com/2026/11/02/seasonal-demand-contributes-to-some-relief-as-four-week-declines-persist-nielseniq/)

### Barrel and closure maker Oeneo: H1 turnover down 5.1%, barrels down 15.8%
_T1b · Oeneo H1 release · 3 Nov_

First-half turnover was €133.1M, down 5.1% (−7.2% at constant scope and exchange rates). Closures were €102.1M (−1.3%). Winemaking, which includes barrels, was €31.0M (−15.8%), with barrels and large vats hit hardest as wine and spirits producers delay investment. Oeneo sees no clear sign of market recovery.

> **Why it matters:** Producers cutting barrel orders now means less new whiskey and oaked wine coming to market in future years.

Sources: [Oeneo release (Actusnews)](https://www.actusnews.com/fr/oeneo/cp/2026/11/03/first-half-turnover-eur-133_1-million-sector-challenges-continue-to-weigh-on-business)
