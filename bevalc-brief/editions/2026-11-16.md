## Top 5

### Hemp-THC: Illinois's ban is in force; New Jersey liquor stores must stop selling hemp drinks from 14 Nov; federal cutoff 11 Dec
_T1 · Illinois Dept. of Revenue · NJ Division of Taxation · Axios · Chicago Sun-Times · CNN · Forbes · CSP · Marijuana Moment · Oct – 13 Nov_

Illinois began enforcing the Illinois Hemp Act on 12 November "regardless of federal timing." Hemp products over 0.4 mg total THC per container, drinks included, can now only be sold by licensed dispensaries, and the Department of Revenue warns of seizure plus tax, penalty and interest. Binny's and other liquor stores cleared THC drinks ahead of the date, per Axios. Hemp businesses asked a court to block the law, per the Chicago Sun-Times, but no ruling has been reported. New today: New Jersey's Division of Taxation says that from 14 November wholesalers and liquor stores may no longer sell intoxicating hemp beverages, which moves to licensed cannabis retailers. Federally, CNN reports synthetic cannabinoids lost hemp status on 12 November, and everything over 0.4 mg follows on 11 December. Marijuana Moment reports that lobbyists for Anheuser-Busch, Molson Coors and Diageo urged state attorneys general to oppose a federal delay. WSWA favors regulation over a ban.

> **Why it matters:** Illinois and New Jersey accounts must now be clear of hemp-THC drinks, so distributors should be processing returns. Every other state follows on 11 December.

Sources: [Illinois Dept. of Revenue bulletin FY 2027-04](https://tax.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/tax/research/publications/bulletins/documents/2027/fy-2027-04.pdf) · [NJ Division of Taxation](https://nj.gov/treasury/taxation/hempbev.shtml) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/chicago/2026/11/05/thc-drinks-illinois-liquor-stores-ban) · [Chicago Sun-Times](https://chicago.suntimes.com/small-business/2026/11/07/chicago-businesses-bracing-state-hemp-thc-ban) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/03/health/federal-hemp-thc-product-ban-wellness) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ajherrington/2026/11/03/federal-hemp-thc-ban-forces-beverage-makers-to-prepare/) · [CSP Daily News](https://www.cspdailynews.com/cbdhemp/illinois-retailers-still-face-nov-12-deadline-pull-hemp-derived-thc-beverages) · [Marijuana Moment](https://www.marijuanamoment.net/alcohol-lobbyists-encouraged-law-enforcement-officials-to-push-for-federal-hemp-crackdown-report-says/)

### **NEW TODAY** IWSR: global beverage alcohol volumes fell 1% in the first half, dragged down by the US and China
_T3 · IWSR via The Spirits Business · 10 Nov · HEADLINE ONLY_

IWSR's first-half 2026 data shows total beverage alcohol consumption across its tracked markets fell 1%. The US and China drove the decline.

### SipSource: spirits depletions stabilizing, wine still under pressure
_T1 · SipSource (WSWA) via Shanken News Daily · 11 Nov_

For the three months through September, spirits depletions fell 3.1% in volume and 3.5% in revenue. Wine did worse, down 6.7% in volume and 4.2% in revenue.

> **Why it matters:** This is distributor-to-retail depletion data, so it shows what wholesalers are actually moving. Spirits declines are narrowing while wine keeps falling faster.

Sources: [Shanken News Daily](https://www.shankennewsdaily.com/2026/11/11/41249/sipsource-spirits-stabilizing-wine-still-under-pressure/)

### Stoli returns to the US with MHW as importer and Empire Merchants and Johnson Brothers among its distributors
_T3 · Drinks Intel · 6–11 Nov_

Stoli Group says it is returning to the US with its full portfolio, including Stoli vodka, Kah tequila, Bayou rum and Writers' Tears, working with its longstanding distribution partners and with stock already in the country. New this week: Drinks Intel reports MHW will be importer of record, with inventory already in bonded US warehouses, and that Stoli will work with regional wholesalers including Empire Merchants in New York and Johnson Brothers, with talks under way with others. It also reports Stoli Group reached a settlement with Stoli Group USA's creditors, which clears the way for US supply to resume.

> **Why it matters:** Distributors and retailers that dropped Stoli brands may see them offered again. Confirm availability with your distributor before resetting catalogs.

Sources: [Drinks Intel: return](https://drinks-intel.com/spirits/stoli-group-returning-to-us/) · [Drinks Intel: MHW and distributors](https://drinks-intel.com/?p=212290)

## Beer & RTD

### **NEW TODAY** AB InBev puts more money into Cutwater production, including $1.5M at its San Diego plant
_T3 · Just Drinks · Shanken News Daily · The Spirits Business · 12–16 Nov_

AB InBev is investing $1.5 million in the San Diego plant that makes Cutwater's cream-based cocktails, part of more than $5 million in Cutwater production spending. Jake Kirsch, president of its Beyond Beer unit, called Cutwater "the number-one spirits-based cocktail brand in the US." New on 13 Nov: Shanken reports Cutwater's dollar sales grew 13% in the four weeks to 31 October, and frames the investment as building Cutwater's innovation hub as RTD competition intensifies. The Spirits Business adds that the money expands cream-based cocktail production ahead of the holidays and will create new manufacturing jobs at the plant, which already employs more than 200 people.

> **Why it matters:** A-B keeps adding capacity for spirits-based RTDs, which its beer wholesalers carry.

Sources: [Just Drinks](https://just-drinks.com/news/ab-inbev-invests-again-in-cutwater-production) · [Shanken News Daily](https://www.shankennewsdaily.com/2026/11/13/41266/a-b-investing-in-cutwaters-innovation-hub-as-rtd-competition-continues-to-intensify/) · [The Spirits Business](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2026/11/ab-inbev-pours-1-5m-into-cutwater-production/)
