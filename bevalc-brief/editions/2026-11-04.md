## Top 5

### **NEW TODAY** Hemp-THC: Illinois enforces its own ban 12 Nov; federal cutoff 11 Dec (synthetics 12 Nov); NJ bill awaits governor
_T1 · Illinois Dept. of Revenue · CNN · NJ 101.5 · Forbes · CSP · Marijuana Moment · Oct – 4 Nov · NEW SOURCE_

Illinois's Department of Revenue says it will start enforcing the Illinois Hemp Act on 12 November "regardless of federal timing." Hemp-THC drinks over 0.4 mg per container must leave mainstream retail and can only be sold through licensed dispensaries. The bulletin warns of product seizure plus tax, penalty and interest, and tells retailers to stop ordering non-compliant products and return existing stock to distributors. CSP reports Illinois c-store chains have stopped reordering. Federally, CNN reports synthetic cannabinoids lose hemp status 12 Nov and everything over 0.4 mg 11 Dec, and another extension is "not out of the question." New Jersey's S4635, which would move its 13 Nov cutoff to the federal date, passed both houses and awaits the governor. Forbes reports producer Crescent Canna is moving more than 700 pallets out of North Carolina. New on 4 Nov: Marijuana Moment, citing emails obtained by Politico, reports that lobbyists for Anheuser-Busch, Molson Coors and Diageo urged state attorneys general to sign the letter asking Congress not to delay the ban. Attorneys general from 34 states and one territory signed it. WSWA, by contrast, favors regulation over a ban.

> **Why it matters:** Illinois accounts have to be cleared by 12 Nov, a month before the federal date, so distributors should plan for returns now. Elsewhere, 12 Nov applies to synthetics and 11 Dec to everything else.

Sources: [Illinois Dept. of Revenue bulletin FY 2027-04](https://tax.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/tax/research/publications/bulletins/documents/2027/fy-2027-04.pdf) · [CSP Daily News](https://www.cspdailynews.com/cbdhemp/illinois-retailers-still-face-nov-12-deadline-pull-hemp-derived-thc-beverages) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/03/health/federal-hemp-thc-product-ban-wellness) · [NJ 101.5](https://nj1015.com/new-jersey-hemp-thc-drinks-deadline/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ajherrington/2026/11/03/federal-hemp-thc-ban-forces-beverage-makers-to-prepare/) · [Marijuana Moment](https://www.marijuanamoment.net/alcohol-lobbyists-encouraged-law-enforcement-officials-to-push-for-federal-hemp-crackdown-report-says/)

### **NEW TODAY** RNDC's liquidation plan approved; KEG 1 closes Kentucky and Columbia closes Alaska
_T3 · Distribution Strategy Group · Brewbound · BevNET · 2 Nov · NEW SOURCE_

The Southern District of Texas bankruptcy court approved RNDC's second amended Chapter 11 liquidation plan on 27 October, which sets up the wind-down without closing the case. KEG 1 River City completed its purchase of RNDC's Kentucky assets effective 30 October, about 2 million cases a year, after Breakthru pulled out. Brewbound reports a joint-venture dispute over Kentucky is still unresolved. Columbia Distributing closed its purchase of RNDC's Alaska assets on 26 October, adding 1,500 accounts.

> **Why it matters:** RNDC's breakup is nearly complete. Kentucky and Alaska suppliers and retailers now have new long-term distributors, and the Kentucky JV dispute is the main open risk.

Sources: [Distribution Strategy Group](https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/11/rndc-wins-court-approval-of-liquidation-plan-as-columbia-closes-alaska-acquisition) · [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/keg-1-completes-rndc-acquisition-as-joint-venture-dispute-lingers) · [BevNET](https://www.bevnet.com/spirits/2026/after-breakthru-walks-keg-1-moves-on-rndcs-kentucky-business)

### Sazerac buys Oregon's Full Sail Brewing for RTD capacity and closes the Hood River brewpub

### Court keeps Constellation's Corona and Modelo with Coors Distributing until arbitration
_T3 · Brewbound · Law360 · 29–30 Oct, 2 Nov_

Coors Distributing Co. won a preliminary injunction that holds Constellation Brands' planned exit from its portfolio until the two companies arbitrate. Constellation tried to end the 18-year agreement without cause. Coors argued the exit posed a "real risk" that other suppliers would leave too. On 2 Nov, Law360 reported Coors and a supplier agreed to send the dispute to an arbitrator.

> **Why it matters:** This is a live test of how freely suppliers can move brands between wholesalers while distributor networks are being reshuffled.

Sources: [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/judge-orders-constellation-brands-and-coors-distributing-company-to-arbitration/) · [Law360: injunction](https://www.law360.com/articles/2544167) · [Law360: arbitration](https://www.law360.com/retail/articles/2545252)

### A-B's Whitworth exits as AB InBev leans on non-beer growth
_T3 · The Drinks Business · AB InBev Q3 6-K · 3 Nov_

Brendan Whitworth leaves at the end of November to become CEO of Equinox, and AB InBev CEO Michel Doukeris will run North America in the interim. ### US to end South Africa's AGOA duty-free access on 1 Jan 2027; wine among exports hit
_T2 · Engineering News · 2 Nov_

US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer said South Africa and Rwanda will be suspended from AGOA from 1 January 2027. South African wine already faces a 30% US tariff. Packaged wine exports to the US fell 21% in volume and 23% in value in 2025, per Wines of South Africa.

> **Why it matters:** Importers of South African wine face another cost increase for 2027. Expect repricing or dropped SKUs.

Sources: [Engineering News: AGOA suspension](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/us-to-suspend-south-africa-and-rwanda-from-duty-free-trade-access-2026-11-02) · [Engineering News: 2025 wine exports](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/south-africas-wine-exports-decrease-2026-01-26)

### **NEW TODAY** North Carolina voters approve expanded alcohol sales in Jones County, Grifton, Pollocksville and Columbus County
_T2 · Public Radio East · WWAY · 4 Nov · NEW SOURCE_

Jones County approved countywide mixed-beverage sales 2,512 to 1,251, covering unincorporated areas and towns that hadn't already authorized them. Grifton and Pollocksville passed their own town measures. Columbus County passed its measures narrowly: mixed beverages 54.56%, malt beverages 50.78% and unfortified wine 50.63%. Businesses still need state permits before they can serve.

> **Why it matters:** These are new on-premise and retail license markets in eastern NC. Expect permit applications from restaurants and c-stores in the coming months.

Sources: [Public Radio East](https://www.publicradioeast.org/2026-11-04/voters-approve-expanded-alcohol-sales-in-grifton-jones-county-and-pollocksville) · [WWAY](https://www.wwaytv3.com/columbus-county-voters-narrowly-approve-expanded-alcohol-sales-in-referendum/)

_T3 · NABCA via The Spirits Business · early Nov_

Volume rose 3.2% and value 1.4% across 18 control states, rebounding from August. NABCA partly credits Labor Day falling in September this year. Montana, Alabama and North Carolina posted double-digit volume gains, while Mississippi, New Hampshire and Wyoming declined.

## Wine

### NielsenIQ: wine still down 3.8% over four weeks, but sparkling turns positive
_T3 · NielsenIQ via Wine Industry Advisor · 2 Nov_

For the four weeks to 24 October, off-premise wine dollars fell 3.8% and volume fell 5.3%. Weaker consumer engagement caused 73% of the losses. Still wine fell 4.6% in value, sparkling rose 1.5% in dollars, and non-alc wine rose 18.4%. Among suppliers, Gallo was down 1.2%, The Wine Group down 9.4% and Deutsch Family up 3.4%.

> **Why it matters:** Sparkling's growth heading into the holidays makes it the category to push in Q4 retailer conversations.

Sources: [Wine Industry Advisor](https://wineindustryadvisor.com/2026/11/02/seasonal-demand-contributes-to-some-relief-as-four-week-declines-persist-nielseniq/)

### Barrel and closure maker Oeneo: H1 turnover down 5.1%, barrels down 15.8%
_T1b · Oeneo H1 release · 3 Nov_

First-half turnover was €133.1M, down 5.1% (−7.2% at constant scope and exchange rates). Closures were €102.1M (−1.3%). Winemaking, which includes barrels, was €31.0M (−15.8%), with barrels and large vats hit hardest as wine and spirits producers delay investment. Oeneo sees no clear sign of market recovery.

> **Why it matters:** Producers cutting barrel orders now means less new whiskey and oaked wine coming to market in future years.

Sources: [Oeneo release (Actusnews)](https://www.actusnews.com/fr/oeneo/cp/2026/11/03/first-half-turnover-eur-133_1-million-sector-challenges-continue-to-weigh-on-business)
