# Coverage guide

This guide is drawn from example industry newsletters the user shared (editions of 30 Oct, 2 Nov and 3 Nov 2026; 57 stories). It describes what the brief should look for each run. Source rules in `sources.yaml` and `CLAUDE.md` still apply: verify every item by search, link the original, and use English-language sources only.

## New section: Retail & on-premise

Covers retailers, delivery platforms, restaurant and bar chains, and retailer associations. Include a story only when it affects how beverage alcohol is bought, sold or delivered, or the health of a major account type. Place it after Beer & RTD.

## Research that can't be linked

The newsletters also carry broker research (Citi, Jefferies) and paywalled services (Politico Pro). These can't be opened or linked, so don't cite them. When the same news has public coverage or a company release, use that instead.
