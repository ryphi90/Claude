# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-04-08*
*Source: Provi Messaging Decisions (internal strategy doc, Feb 4, 2026)*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** One place to see what's available across your distributors, plan your orders, and stay organized — without changing the relationships you already rely on.

**What it does:** Provi consolidates all of a bar or restaurant operator's beverage buying needs by bringing every distributor's catalog, rep communication, and order requests into one place. It gives operators visibility into products across all their distributors, centralizes fragmented workflows, and provides tools to organize ordering — so they spend less time chasing down details and more time running their business.

**Product category:** Beverage ordering and catalog management platform for on-premise and off-premise alcohol retailers

**Product type:** SaaS marketplace / B2B workflow platform

**Business model:** Subscription-based platform for retailers; distributor-side integrations (partner and non-partner distributors)

---

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Bars, restaurants, country clubs, and retail liquor stores — any licensed establishment that purchases alcohol from multiple distributors

**Decision-makers:** Owner/operators who manage beverage purchasing and inventory. May also include bar managers and buyers at larger establishments.

**Primary use case:** Managing beverage ordering across multiple distributors — finding products, comparing options, placing orders, and tracking communication — without juggling phone calls, texts, emails, and spreadsheets.

**Jobs to be done:**
- "Help me find what's available across all my distributors without calling every rep"
- "Help me keep track of what I've ordered and what I need to reorder"
- "Help me organize my buying process so I'm not doing it all in my head"

**Use cases:**
- Searching for a product across all distributor catalogs at once
- Building and managing reorder lists and favorites
- Communicating with reps and tracking orders in one inbox
- Planning menu changes or seasonal updates
- Onboarding new staff to existing buying workflows

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem (retailer pain statement):** Distributors don't do things the same way. The reps, catalogs, discounts, relationships, and preferred way of ordering are all different. For the owner/operator managing all of this, they can't see it all in one place. There's no way to compare products, to track down who carries what, or what's been ordered. So they cobble it together through phone calls and texts and emails. Questions that come up after hours and orders that need to be placed have to wait until morning. Time that should go to running the business or relaxing after hours gets swallowed up managing relationships and processes just to keep the shelves full.

**Three core pain themes:**

**1. Lack of Visibility (Awareness)**
- Can't compare products across distributors
- Can't track down who carries what
- Miss potential discount opportunities
- Reactive decision-making instead of proactive planning
- Only see what a rep happens to pitch, what they remember to ask for, or what they already buy

**2. Fragmented Workflow (Action)**
- Information scattered across texts, calls, emails, PDFs, portals, and conversations
- No consistent process across distributors
- Extra mental load to remember different systems
- Time wasted switching tools and repeating themselves
- Questions get lost in the shuffle

**3. Poor Organization (Efficiency)**
- Knowledge trapped in individuals' heads
- Can't delegate or collaborate on ordering
- Hard to onboard new staff
- Product info lives in messy spreadsheets with inconsistent names, formats, or outdated details
- No historical reference for ordering decisions

**What it costs them:** Hours per week managing distributor relationships and processes just to keep shelves stocked. After-hours stress. Missed product opportunities. Error-prone reordering. Inability to delegate or scale.

**Emotional tension:** Feeling reactive, unprepared, overwhelmed. Lack of control over a core business function. The frustration of knowing there's a better way but not having the tools for it.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct competitors:**
- **SevenFifty** — Used by reps (not buyers); serves the distributor side of the relationship. Reps use SevenFifty; buyers use Provi. Some reps are hostile to Provi because of this dynamic.
- **BevLink** — Has data ingestion capability (spreadsheet upload) that Provi currently lacks; this is a meaningful competitive disadvantage for onboarding
- **eOrders** — Competitor with GMV-based functionality; details still being gathered

**Indirect competitors:**
- **Phone/text/email** — For many retailers, traditional methods are still faster due to pricing transparency gaps and familiarity
- **Excel/spreadsheets** — Retailers use complex multi-tab spreadsheets (wine, liquor, beer, mixers, etc.) for ordering management; manual but familiar

**How alternatives fall short:**
- Scattered tools create fragmentation and mental overhead
- No single view across all distributors
- No consolidated search across catalogs
- No ability to organize, save, or delegate buying workflows

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Search across every distributor catalog at once (consolidated catalog access)
- One platform instead of multiple distributor portals, PDFs, and logins
- Unified inbox for rep communication and order requests
- Lists, favorites, and order guides for organized reordering
- 55,000+ reps on the Provi platform

**How we do it differently:** Provi consolidates the fragmented distributor ecosystem into one workflow — without replacing existing rep relationships. Retailers keep their relationships; they just stop juggling 10 different ways of managing them.

**Why that's better:** Operators move from reactive to proactive. They know their options before conversations happen. They can delegate, onboard staff, and plan ahead — instead of carrying everything in their heads.

**Why customers choose us:** Visibility and consolidation. The ability to search every catalog at once and see options across distributors is immediately valuable — users experience this on first use.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already have a system — phone/text/email works fine" | Those methods work, but they keep you reactive. Provi doesn't replace your rep relationships; it gives you the full picture before those conversations happen. |
| "My reps don't use Provi" | Provi isn't about changing how your reps work — it's about giving you visibility into every distributor's catalog so you're prepared regardless of what any one rep shows you. |
| "It takes too long to set up" | This is a real concern — building lists manually can take 1–1.5 hours. We acknowledge the onboarding cost and focus messaging on immediate catalog/search value rather than overpromising setup ease. |
| "I'm not sure it covers my distributors" | Catalog coverage varies by market. Lead with what's confirmed (NY, GA have strong distributor coverage). Avoid claiming "every product" or "complete catalog." |

**Anti-persona:**
- Retailers who primarily work with one or two distributors and have a stable, simple buying process
- Retailers in markets with low distributor coverage where Provi's catalog value is limited
- Buyers who are wine-centric or discovery-focused (currently underserved by product)

## Switching Dynamics

**Push (frustrations with current approach):**
- Wasting time chasing down info across texts, calls, emails
- Missing products or discounts they didn't know existed
- After-hours stress when questions arise and reps are unavailable
- Can't delegate ordering because the process lives in one person's head
- Messy spreadsheets with outdated or inconsistent product data

**Pull (what attracts them to Provi):**
- See every distributor's catalog in one search
- One inbox for all rep communication
- Build lists and reorder guides
- Work from one platform instead of juggling multiple portals

**Habit (what keeps them stuck):**
- Existing rep relationships and communication habits (phone/text)
- Familiar spreadsheets, even if messy
- "Good enough" current process that feels faster in the short term
- Low motivation to invest time in setup/onboarding

**Anxiety (what worries them about switching):**
- "Will my reps be hostile to this?"
- "Will the catalog actually have my products?"
- "Is the setup worth the time investment?"
- "Will this break relationships I've spent years building?"

- Empowering

## Messaging Guardrails

**Core principle: 0% Aspirational Marketing**
All messaging must focus on what Provi does well **today**, not future capabilities.

**Safe to message:**
- Consolidated view of multiple distributor catalogs
- Search across all distributors at once
- One platform instead of multiple portals
- Save products to lists for easy reordering
- Track ordering activity

**Do NOT message:**
- "Every product" or "complete catalog" (coverage varies by market)
- Real-time pricing accuracy
- Seamless order flow across all distributors
- Provi improves rep relationships (it doesn't — it distances buyers from reps)
- Provi Chat heavily until validated
- Ordering speed/efficiency improvements we can't consistently deliver

**Channel split:**
- **External marketing:** Lead with search/catalog visibility only
- **In-platform messaging:** Can reference ordering features with proper context
